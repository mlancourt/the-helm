Privacy policy — LannyAI Mail Attachments

Effective 11 November 2026.

LannyAI Mail Attachments is a private tool with one user: its owner, who is also its developer. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it accesses

With its owner's consent it uses the Gmail API with the read-only scope (gmail.readonly). It reads individual messages in the owner's own Gmail account, and their attachments, only when the owner's filing system has already recorded that message as a document to keep.

What it does with the data

Attachments are saved to the owner's own private storage, beside the record of the email they came from. Nothing else is done with them.

What it does not do

It does not send, modify or delete mail. It does not share, sell or transfer Gmail data to anyone. It does not use Gmail data for advertising or to train AI or machine-learning models. No data is sent to any server operated by the tool; it has none.

Its use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and removal

Saved attachments stay in the owner's archive until the owner deletes them. Access can be withdrawn at any time in the Google Account's third-party access settings, which stops the tool immediately.

Contact

The tool's only user is its owner. Questions can be raised through the contact details shown on the Google consent screen for this app.
